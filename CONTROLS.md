Two-button fallback profile

Temporary mode while GPIO13 / green button is unreliable.

GPIO18  green fire
GPIO19  red fire
GPIO13  disabled

Special should not be triggered by normal double-tap because that conflicts with rapid repeated fire.

Preferred special activation in two-button mode:

hold both buttons together for a configured duration

Pause can be:

short simultaneous press of both buttons

This needs careful timing separation from special hold.
